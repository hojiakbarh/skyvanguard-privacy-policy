Privacy Policy

App: SkyVanguard (com.hojiakbar.skyvanguard) • Effective Date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SkyVanguard ("we", "our", or "the App"), published under package identifier com.hojiakbar.skyvanguard, collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you install and play the game on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

SkyVanguard collects only the minimum data strictly necessary to provide cloud account sign-in, save game progress across devices, and power online leaderboards and 1v1 multiplayer matches:

  • Account & Authentication Data (Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication): When you sign in with Google, we receive your unique Firebase User ID (uid), basic profile display name, and email address solely to authenticate your pilot account and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Gameplay Progress & Leaderboard Data (Cloud Firestore): We store your chosen in-game pilot callsign, virtual credits, unlocked aircraft and camouflage skins, subsystem upgrade levels, match scores, win/kill statistics, and temporary 1v1 PvP room state so your progress is preserved if you reinstall the app or switch devices.
  • Basic Network & Diagnostic State: Standard network connectivity status is checked locally to synchronize cloud saves and online rooms reliably.
What We Do NOT Collect: SkyVanguard does not access or collect your GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, photos/files, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third parties.

2. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Required for Google Sign-In, cloud profile synchronization, leaderboards, and 1v1 PvP multiplayer.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to verify active internet connectivity before cloud operations.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Used exclusively for in-game tactile haptic feedback during flight and combat events.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses trusted Google platform infrastructure to operate authentication and cloud database features. These services process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between SkyVanguard and Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS (SSL). Database access is restricted by server-side Firebase Security Rules so that only the authenticated account owner can modify their own pilot profile.

5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

We retain your cloud game profile for as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request complete deletion of your SkyVanguard account and all associated personal and gameplay data (Firebase Auth record, pilot profile, and leaderboard entry) at any time:

How to request account and data deletion:

  • Send an email from the Google address linked to your game account to hojiakbarpy@gmail.com with the subject "SkyVanguard Account Deletion Request".
  • We will permanently delete your authentication record, saved pilot profile, and leaderboard data within 7 business days and confirm completion by email.

6. Children's Privacy

SkyVanguard does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal data, please contact us at hojiakbarpy@gmail.com so we can promptly delete such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy or SkyVanguard, please contact the developer at:
Email: hojiakbarpy@gmail.com
